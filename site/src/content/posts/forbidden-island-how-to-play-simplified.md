## What's In The Box

- 24 Island tiles (each with a flooded reverse side)
- 4 treasure figures (Earth Stone, Statue of the Wind, Crystal of Fire, Ocean's Chalice)
- Treasure deck (including Helicopter Lift and Sandbag cards)
- Flood deck
- 6 Adventurer role cards, each with a matching pawn
- Water Level meter and marker

![The Forbidden Island tiles laid out as the island](https://img.hexagamers.com/Forbidden-Island-Tiles_t7lwwp.webp "The Forbidden Island board")

## Setup

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div>Shuffle the 24 Island tiles and lay them face-up in the island shape (a diamond, wider in the middle). All pawns start on <strong>Fool's Landing</strong>.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div>Place the 4 treasure figures on their matching tiles.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">3</span><div>Set the Water Level marker to your chosen difficulty (Novice through Legendary).</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">4</span><div>Shuffle the Flood deck, draw the top 6 cards, and flip the matching tiles to their flooded side.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">5</span><div>Shuffle the 6 role cards and deal one to each player. Deal each player 2 Treasure cards to start.</div></div>
</div>

![The adventurer role cards in Forbidden Island](https://img.hexagamers.com/Forbidden-Island-Roles_rhy2t4.webp "Forbidden Island role cards")

## The Roles

<div class="rule-cards">
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Pilot</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Fly Anywhere</p>
    <p>Once per turn, fly your pawn to any tile on the board — even one that's not adjacent.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Navigator</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Move Others</p>
    <p>Move another player's pawn up to 2 adjacent tiles, for one action.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Explorer</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Diagonal Movement</p>
    <p>Move and shore up tiles diagonally, not just up-down-left-right.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Diver</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Swim Through Flooded Tiles</p>
    <p>Move through any number of adjacent flooded or missing tiles in a single action.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Engineer</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Two Shores, One Action</p>
    <p>Shore up two adjacent tiles for the cost of a single action.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Messenger</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Give Cards Remotely</p>
    <p>Hand a Treasure card to any other player regardless of where either of you is standing.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## How a Turn Works

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div><strong>Take up to 3 actions</strong>, in any combination: move to an adjacent tile, shore up a flooded tile back to normal, give a Treasure card to a player on your tile, or capture a treasure.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div><strong>Draw 2 Treasure cards.</strong> If you draw a <strong>Waters Rise!</strong> card, raise the water level, reshuffle the flood discard pile on top of the flood deck, and discard the card — it doesn't go to your hand.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">3</span><div><strong>Draw Flood cards</strong> equal to the current water level. Flip the matching tiles to flooded, or remove them entirely if they're already flooded.</div></div>
</div>

**Capturing a treasure** costs one action, requires 4 matching Treasure cards, and can only happen on one of that treasure's two designated tiles.

## Sandbags and the Helicopter Lift

Two card types don't wait for your turn:

**Sandbag cards** can be played any time, by anyone, to shore up a flooded tile before it's removed for good. No action required.

**Helicopter Lift cards** move any number of pawns from one tile to any other tile on the board, at any time. Once your team holds all 4 treasures, everyone gathers on Fool's Landing and discards a Helicopter Lift card to fly off the island — that's the win.

![The water level meter that sets Forbidden Island's difficulty](https://img.hexagamers.com/Forbidden-Island-Water-level_iwzxwz.webp "The Forbidden Island water meter")

## How to Win

Collect all 4 treasures, get every pawn to Fool's Landing, and discard a Helicopter Lift card. That's it.

## How to Lose

You lose immediately if any of these happen:
- Both tiles for a treasure sink before you've captured it
- Fool's Landing sinks
- A player's tile sinks and there's no adjacent tile to move to (Diver, Explorer, and Pilot have workarounds)
- The Water Level marker reaches the skull and crossbones

![The four treasures players race to collect in Forbidden Island](https://img.hexagamers.com/Forbidden-Island-Items_drubty.webp "The treasures in Forbidden Island")

<div class="faq">
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>What happens if Fool's Landing floods?</summary>
    <p>You can still win as long as someone shores it up before it's drawn again from the flood deck. If a second Fool's Landing flood card comes up before it's repaired, the game ends immediately.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Can I shore up a tile that's already sunk?</summary>
    <p>No. Once a tile is removed from the board, it's gone for the rest of the game. Only flooded (but not yet sunk) tiles can be shored up.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Do I have to use all 3 of my actions?</summary>
    <p>No. You can take fewer than 3, though there's rarely a reason not to use them all when the island is actively sinking under you.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Can two players have the same role?</summary>
    <p>No. Each of the 6 role cards is dealt once, so with fewer than 6 players some roles simply won't be in play that game.</p>
  </details>
</div>
