You're a Renaissance merchant collecting gem tokens, using them to buy gem cards, then using those cards to buy even better ones on your way to 15 prestige points. Splendor has almost no text and no fiddly rules, which makes it one of the easiest real strategy games to teach. You can have everyone playing in about five minutes.

> **Note:** This is a condensed version of the rules to get you playing fast — nothing important is left out, but read the full rulebook for every edge case.

## What's In The Box

- 40 gem tokens in 5 colours (emerald, sapphire, ruby, diamond, onyx) + 5 gold (joker)
- 90 development cards in 3 tiers
- 10 noble tiles

## Setup

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div>Shuffle the three card tiers separately and lay them in <strong>three rows</strong> (Tier 1 on the bottom). Reveal <strong>4 cards in each row</strong>.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div>Shuffle the nobles and reveal a number equal to <strong>players + 1</strong> (so 4 nobles for a 3-player game).</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">3</span><div>Place the <strong>gem tokens</strong> in 5 colour stacks. Use <strong>7 of each</strong> for 4 players, <strong>5 each</strong> for 3 players, <strong>4 each</strong> for 2 players. Always set out all 5 gold tokens.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">4</span><div>Youngest player goes first. Players start with no tokens and no cards.</div></div>
</div>

## How a Turn Works

On your turn you take **exactly one** of these four actions:

<div class="rule-cards">
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">ACTION A</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Take 3 different gems</p>
    <p>Take one token each of three different colours.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">ACTION B</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Take 2 of the same gem</p>
    <p>Only allowed if that colour's stack still has at least 4 tokens.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">ACTION C</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Buy a card</p>
    <p>Pay its gem cost (using tokens and/or your card bonuses) to take a face-up card or a previously reserved one.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">ACTION D</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Reserve a card</p>
    <p>Take any face-up card (or the top of a deck) into your hand for later and grab 1 gold token. Max 3 reserved at a time.</p>
  </div>
</div>

> **Hand limit:** if you ever end your turn holding more than **10 tokens**, return tokens until you're back down to 10.

## The Engine: Cards Become Discounts

This is the one idea that makes Splendor click. Every card you buy shows a **gem bonus** in its corner. That bonus acts as a **permanent discount** on the cost of future cards.

Buy a card with a green bonus, and from then on every card you buy costs one less green gem — paid by the card, not your tokens. Stack a few bonuses and expensive cards start costing you almost nothing. Gold tokens are wild and can stand in for any one gem.

## Nobles

Nobles visit you automatically. At the **end of your turn**, if your card bonuses meet a noble's requirement (e.g. 3 emerald + 3 ruby cards), that noble comes to you for free, worth **3 prestige points**. You can only gain one noble per turn, and you never spend anything for them.

## How to Win

Play continues until a player reaches **15 prestige points**. Finish the current round so everyone has had an equal number of turns, then the highest score wins.

- Points are printed on the more valuable development cards.
- Each noble is worth 3 points.
- **Tiebreaker:** the player who bought the **fewest development cards** wins.

<div class="faq">
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Can I take 2 of the same gem from a nearly empty stack?</summary>
    <p>No. You may only take 2 of one colour if that stack has at least 4 tokens left before you take them. Otherwise, take 3 different colours instead.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>What are gold tokens for?</summary>
    <p>Gold is a wild gem — it can substitute for any one colour when buying a card. You only get gold by reserving a card, which makes reserving a sneaky way to bank flexible currency.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Do card bonuses run out?</summary>
    <p>Never. Once a card is in front of you, its gem bonus is a permanent discount for the rest of the game. That's why your purchases speed up dramatically late-game.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Do I have to do anything to earn a noble?</summary>
    <p>No — nobles are checked automatically at the end of each turn. If your bonuses meet the requirement, the noble (and 3 points) is yours for free.</p>
  </details>
</div>
