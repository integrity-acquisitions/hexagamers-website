Your helicopter went down in an ancient desert, and the only way out is to rebuild it. Excavate sand, track down four hidden flying machine parts, and manage your water supply before the storm buries the whole board — or the heat does you in first. Here's the whole game in about 5 minutes, nothing important left out.

## What's In The Box

- 24 Desert tiles (arranged in a 5×5 grid with a gap in the middle — the eye of the storm)
- 4 Flying Machine parts: propeller, engine, compass, solar crystal
- Sand markers (light and dark)
- Storm deck
- 6 Adventurer role cards
- Water Level dials (one per player) and the Storm Meter

![The Forbidden Desert sand tiles laid out for play](https://img.hexagamers.com/Forbidden-Desert-Tiles_kxxqvc.webp "The Forbidden Desert board")

## Setup

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div>Lay the 24 tiles face-down in a 5×5 grid, leaving the center space empty — that gap is the <strong>eye of the storm</strong>.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div>Place a Sand marker (light side up) on each of the 8 tiles marked with a sun symbol.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">3</span><div>Set the 4 Flying Machine parts aside — each is found by excavating the two tiles that mark its latitude and longitude.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">4</span><div>Set the Storm Meter to your chosen difficulty. It starts most games at level 2.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">5</span><div>Shuffle the 6 role cards and deal one to each player, along with the matching Water Level dial set to that role's starting water.</div></div>
</div>

## How a Turn Works

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div><strong>Take up to 4 actions</strong>, in any combination: move to an adjacent tile, clear 1 Sand marker from your tile, excavate a tile (flip it face-up once it has no sand on it — this can reveal a machine part locator), pick up a part, drink from your canteen to refill water at a Well, or use your role's special ability.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div><strong>Draw Storm cards</strong> equal to the current Storm Meter level (starts at 2, climbs as the game goes on).</div></div>
</div>

**Storm cards come in three flavors.** A **Sun Beats Down** card costs every exposed player 1 water (a Tunnel tile shelters you from it). A **Storm Picks Up** card raises the Storm Meter a notch, meaning more cards next turn. A **movement card** shows a number and a direction — that many tiles slide toward the storm's empty center, and every tile that moves gains a fresh Sand marker.

![Sand tiles piling up as the storm blows in Forbidden Desert](https://img.hexagamers.com/Forbidden-Desert-Sand-Tiles-2_qyaukp.webp "Shifting sand tiles in Forbidden Desert")

**Locating a part takes two tiles, not one.** Each of the 4 Flying Machine parts has two locator tiles — one marks its row, one marks its column. Excavate both, and the part appears where they intersect. Because the storm keeps shuffling tiles around, that intersection point moves too.

![Tracking each player's water supply in Forbidden Desert](https://img.hexagamers.com/Forbidden-Desert-Water_foizuv.webp "Water management in Forbidden Desert")

## How to Win

Recover all 4 Flying Machine parts, get every player to the Launch Pad tile (cleared of sand), and play a Flying Machine card to escape.

## How to Lose

You lose immediately if any of these happen:
- Any player's water reaches 0
- The Storm Meter reaches the skull and crossbones
- The desert runs out of Sand markers to place

<div class="faq">
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>How do I refill my water?</summary>
    <p>Move to a Well tile (once excavated) and spend an action to fill your canteen. The Water Carrier can also share water with anyone on their tile.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>What stops me from losing water to Sun Beats Down cards?</summary>
    <p>Standing on an excavated Tunnel tile when a Sun Beats Down card is drawn protects you from that water loss.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Can I excavate a tile that still has sand on it?</summary>
    <p>No. A tile can only be flipped face-up (excavated) once every Sand marker on it has been cleared.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>What happens to a machine part if the tile under it shifts?</summary>
    <p>It moves with the tile. Parts, pawns, and Sand markers all travel along with their tile whenever a storm movement card shifts the board.</p>
  </details>
</div>
