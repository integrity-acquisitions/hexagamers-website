Flip 7: With a Vengeance is a push-your-luck card game: flip number cards one at a time, avoid collecting a duplicate, and decide each turn whether to keep going or bank what you have. Collect seven different numbers and the round ends instantly with a bonus. This is the standalone sequel to Flip 7, with a bigger number deck and a stack of new cards that let you mess with other players' hands. Here's the whole game in about five minutes — nothing important left out.

> Our full review: [Flip 7: With a Vengeance Review](/flip-7-with-a-vengeance-review/) · New to the series? [Flip 7 Review](/flip-7-review/)

> **Note:** This is a condensed version of the rules to get you playing fast — nothing important is left out, but read the full rulebook for every edge case.

## What's In The Box

- Number cards 1–13 (one 1, two 2s, three 3s, up to thirteen 13s)
- Action cards: Flip Four, Take One More and Freeze, Swap, Steal, Discard
- Special number cards: Lucky 13, Unlucky 7, and 0
- Modifier cards: point bonuses, subtractors, and a divide-by-2 card

## Setup

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div>Shuffle the full deck and pick a dealer for the round.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div>Deal <strong>one card face up</strong> to every player, including the dealer.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">3</span><div>If anyone's starting card is an action card, resolve it immediately before play begins.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">4</span><div>The player to the dealer's left goes first, then play moves clockwise.</div></div>
</div>

## How a Turn Works

On your turn, choose one of two options:

<div class="rule-cards">
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Option 1</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Hit</p>
    <p>Take one more card from the dealer and add it face up in front of you.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Option 2</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Stay</p>
    <p>Turn your leftmost card sideways and lock in your current total for the round. You can't hit again, but you can still be targeted by other players' action cards.</p>
  </div>
</div>

**Busting.** If you ever have two number cards of the same value in front of you, you bust. Flip your cards face down and sit out the rest of the round — you score nothing for it.

**Flipping 7.** If you collect seven different number cards without busting, the round ends immediately. You score a bonus 15 points on top of everything else.

## The Special Number Cards

<div class="rule-cards">
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Zero</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Score Nothing — Unless You Push</p>
    <p>Your total for the round becomes 0 unless you go on to flip seven different numbers. Once you hold a 0, you must keep hitting each turn until you either flip 7 or bust. It still counts toward your seven-card total.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Lucky 13</p>
    <p class="rule-title">A Second 13, No Bust</p>
    <p>Normally two 13s would bust you. Lucky 13 lets you hold a second 13 instead — both count toward your score and toward flipping 7. A third 13 still busts you.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Unlucky 7</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Everything Goes but This</p>
    <p>Discard every number and modifier card in front of you and keep only the Unlucky 7. Any 7 you draw after this busts you as normal.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## The Action Cards

Action cards are resolved the instant they're drawn or dealt — **you must use one if a valid target exists.**

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Flip Four** | The target flips up to four cards, one at a time. Stop immediately if they duplicate a number or complete a Flip 7. |
| **Take One More and Freeze** | The target takes one more card, then is locked into Stay for the rest of the round. |
| **Swap** | Force any two players to trade one face-up card each. |
| **Steal** | Take one face-up card from another player's tableau and add it to your own. |
| **Discard** | Force a player to discard one card from their tableau. |

Watch for chain reactions — a Swap or Steal can hand someone a duplicate number and bust them on the spot, even if they'd already chosen to Stay.

## Modifier Cards

Modifier cards don't count toward your seven-card total, and they can be swapped or discarded like any other card.

- **Bonus modifiers** add flat points to a hand's final score.
- **Negative modifiers** subtract points — your score can't go below 0 in the standard game.
- **Divide by 2** halves your total round score before other modifiers are applied, rounding down.

The twist: several of these are built to hand off. Instead of keeping a modifier for yourself, you can give a negative one straight to another player's hand, cutting into their score instead of building your own.

## Scoring a Round

When everyone has either stayed, busted, or the round ended on a Flip 7, tally each player's score:

1. Add up the value of all number cards still in front of you.
2. Apply a divide-by-2 modifier first, if you have one (round down).
3. Apply any remaining bonus or negative modifiers.
4. Add 15 points if you flipped 7.

Busted players score 0 for the round. Set all cards aside and move to the next round with a new dealer.

## Winning the Game

Play continues across rounds until, at the end of a round, at least one player has reached **200 points or more**. Whoever has the highest score at that point wins the game.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq">
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Can I be targeted by an action card after I've already chosen to Stay?</summary>
    <p>Yes. Staying locks you out of hitting on your own, but you're still a legal target for Swap, Steal, Discard, and Take One More and Freeze.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>What happens if a Swap or Steal gives me a duplicate number?</summary>
    <p>You bust immediately, the same as if you'd drawn the duplicate yourself. Flip your cards face down and sit out the rest of the round.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>Do modifier cards count toward my seven-card Flip 7 total?</summary>
    <p>No. Only number cards count toward flipping seven different numbers. Modifiers just adjust your final score.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq-item">
    <summary>What happens if the deck runs out mid-round?</summary>
    <p>Shuffle the discard pile into a new deck and keep playing.</p>
  </details>
</div>
