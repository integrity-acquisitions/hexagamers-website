That's the heart of both **Ticket to Ride** (the original, North America) and **Ticket to Ride: Europe**, and it's why so many people search for rules to either one. They share a core engine but play differently enough that we cover them both here, in full, on one page.

## Ticket to Ride (Original)

[![Ticket to Ride](https://img.hexagamers.com/hexagamers-box-art/ticket-to-ride.webp)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F8PKN4B7/?tag=hexagamers-20)

<div class="game-stats">
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat-value">2 – 5</span><span class="stat-label">Players</span></div>
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat-value">30–60 min</span><span class="stat-label">Play Time</span></div>
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat-value">8+</span><span class="stat-label">Age</span></div>
</div>

The original game everything else in the series is built on. Players race to connect cities across a map of North America, and the game is a shade simpler than Europe — no stations, no tunnels, no ferries, just cards, routes and a fight over who gets the good double-routes.

> **Note:** This guide covers the core rules to get you started — not every edge case. Grab the [official rules PDF](https://ncdn0.daysofwonder.com/tickettoride/en/img/tt_rules_2015_en.pdf) from the publisher for full detail.

### The Objective

Score the most points by claiming routes between cities, completing the Destination Tickets you keep, and — if you can manage it — building the single longest continuous path of trains on the board. Any Destination Ticket you fail to complete by the end of the game subtracts its value from your score instead of adding it.

### Setup

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div>Lay out the board. Each player takes a set of 45 Train Cars in one colour and places their scoring marker on "Start."</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div>Deal each player <strong>4 Train Car cards</strong> to start their hand. Turn the <strong>top 5 cards</strong> of the remaining deck face-up beside it.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">3</span><div>Deal each player <strong>3 Destination Ticket cards</strong>. Players must keep at least 2 — return any others face-down to the bottom of the deck.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">4</span><div>Place the Longest Path Bonus card face-up next to the board.</div></div>
</div>

### On Your Turn — Choose 1 Action

<div class="rule-cards">
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Action 1</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Draw Train Car Cards</p>
    <p>Take 2 cards from the face-up display or the draw pile (your choice, mix and match). If you take a face-up Locomotive (wild card), that counts as both draws — you get only 1 card total that turn.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Action 2</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Claim a Route</p>
    <p>Play a set of matching Train Car cards equal to the number of spaces between two cities, then place your trains on it. The route is yours — no one else can use it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Action 3</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Draw Destination Tickets</p>
    <p>Draw 3 new Destination Ticket cards and keep at least 1. Whatever you keep and don't finish costs you points at game end.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Double-Routes

Some cities are connected by two parallel routes side by side. In a **4 or 5 player game**, both are open and two different players can each claim one. In a **2 or 3 player game**, only one of the two can ever be used — once someone claims either route, the other is closed for the rest of the game.

### Scoring

**During the game** — score points immediately when you claim a route, based on its length:

| Route Length | Points |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 7 |
| 5 | 10 |
| 6 | 15 |

**End of game bonuses:**
- Completed Destination Tickets: **+card value**
- Uncompleted Destination Tickets: **−card value**
- Longest continuous path of trains: **+10 points** (ties both score the bonus)

### End of Game

When any player has **2 or fewer trains left** at the end of their turn, every other player — including that player — gets one final turn. Then reveal Destination Tickets and total all points. Highest score wins; ties go to whoever completed the most Destination Tickets, then whoever holds the Longest Path bonus.

## Ticket to Ride: Europe

<div class="game-stats">
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat-value">2 – 5</span><span class="stat-label">Players</span></div>
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat-value">30–60 min</span><span class="stat-label">Play Time</span></div>
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat-value">8+</span><span class="stat-label">Age</span></div>
</div>

Connect cities across Europe with coloured trains to complete routes and score points. Longer routes are worth more points. But watch out — rivals may be racing for the same cities, forcing you to find alternate paths. Europe adds train stations, tunnels and ferries on top of the original's core rules — see how those differ from the original game above.

> Full thoughts here: [Ticket to Ride: Europe – Review](/ticket-to-ride-europe-review/) · Buying your first box? [Ticket to Ride vs Ticket to Ride Europe](/ticket-to-ride-vs-ticket-to-ride-europe/) · [Every Ticket to Ride Version Ranked](/ticket-to-ride-versions-ranked/)

> **Note:** This guide covers the core rules to get you started — not every edge case. For the full detail, grab the [official rules PDF](/ticket-to-ride-europe-rules-pdf/) — we link the publisher's own rulebook alongside a complete rules summary.

### The Objective

Connect two cities on a Destination Route card using a chain of coloured train segments on the board. Completing routes earns points. Failing to complete a route by the end of the game costs you points equal to its value.

### Setup

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">1</span><div>Lay out the game board. Each player takes their trains, train stations, and score marker.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">2</span><div>Deal each player <strong>4 Destination Route cards</strong> (1 long, 3 short). Players must keep at least 2 — return any they don't want, face-down.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">3</span><div>Deal each player <strong>3 Train Car cards</strong> to start their hand.</div></div>
  <div class="step"><span class="step-num">4</span><div>Place <strong>5 Train Car cards face-up</strong> in the centre. The remainder form a face-down draw pile beside them.</div></div>
</div>

### On Your Turn — Choose 1 Action

<div class="rule-cards">
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Action 1</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Draw Train Car Cards</p>
    <p>Take 2 cards from the face-up display or draw pile (your choice). If you take a face-up Locomotive (wild card), that counts as both draws — you get only 1 card total.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Action 2</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Claim a Route</p>
    <p>Play matching Train Car cards equal to the number of spaces between two cities. The route is now yours — no one else can use it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Action 3</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Draw Route Cards</p>
    <p>Draw 3 Destination Route cards and keep at least 1. Uncompleted routes count as negative points at game end.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="rule-card">
    <p class="rule-num">Action 4</p>
    <p class="rule-title">Place a Train Station</p>
    <p>Costs Train Car cards (1 card for your 1st station, 2 for your 2nd, 3 for your 3rd). A station lets you use one of another player's routes to complete your own destination.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Special Route Types

| Route Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Grey** | Use any single colour — all cards played must match each other |
| **Ferry** | Requires at least 1 (sometimes more) Locomotive card to complete |
| **Underground (Tunnel)** | After playing your cards, flip 3 from the draw pile — if any match your colour (including Locomotives), you must play extra cards or abandon the route |

### Scoring

**During the game** — when you claim a route, score points immediately based on length:

| Route Length | Points |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 7 |
| 6 | 15 |
| 8 | 21 |

**End of game bonuses:**
- Completed Destination Route cards: **+card value**
- Uncompleted Destination Route cards: **−card value**
- Longest continuous train: **+10 points** (train stations don't count toward this)
- Each unused train station remaining: **+4 points**

### End of Game

When any player has **2 or fewer trains left**, finish the current round so everyone gets one more turn. Then total all points.
